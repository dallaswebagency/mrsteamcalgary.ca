Questions, answered

Pricing and booking

How do I get a price?

Build an estimate on our Book Online page, or call or text (587) 899-3890 and we'll help you work it out. Prices there are starting prices, and we confirm the details with you before the cleaning.

Is there a minimum charge?

Yes, the minimum service call is $199. If your selected items add up to less, the minimum applies.

How do I book?

Use the Book Online page, fill out the quick form on our contact page, or call or text (587) 899-3890. Your request is confirmed once we reply with a time.

Are quotes really free?

Yes. Quotes are free and come with no obligation.

Can I send photos?

Yes. You can add photos when you book online or in the quote form. They help us understand stains, size and fabric.

Safety

Are your products safe for pets and kids?

Yes. We use eco-friendly products that are safe for pets and kids. Tell us about your family and furry friends when you book and we'll plan around them.

Are your products eco-friendly?

Yes. We use cleaning products that are kinder to your family, pets and the environment.

The cleaning

What if I have heavy pet hair, stains or odours?

Choose Heavy Duty on the booking page for those items. We also offer stain removal and pet odour treatment. Text us a photo if you'd like us to take a look first.

What do you clean?

Carpets, upholstery (sofas, sectionals, loveseats and chairs) and area rugs of every size.

Do you clean businesses and apartments?

We do. Steam Genie cleans homes, apartments and commercial spaces. Call or text for a commercial quote.

Do I need to prepare anything?

Point out problem spots when you book, tell us about pets, and move small items out of the way. We will confirm any other details when we reply.
