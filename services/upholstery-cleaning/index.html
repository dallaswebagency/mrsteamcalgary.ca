What your furniture care tag means

Look for a small tag under a cushion or along the seam. It is a quick way to know how your sofa likes to be cleaned.

Tag codeWhat it meansWhat to expect
WWater-based cleaning is fineGreat fit for steam cleaning
SSolvent-based cleaner onlyTell us before booking so we plan around it
WSWater or solvent is fineUsually the easiest to clean
XVacuum only, no liquidsText us a photo and we will advise

Before we arrive

A few easy dos and don'ts

Please do

  • Vacuum crumbs and pet hair from cushions
  • Blot fresh spills with a clean, dry cloth
  • Text us photos of stains and the care tag
  • Tell us about pets and allergies

Please avoid

  • Scrubbing stains, which spreads them
  • Using bleach or strong sprays
  • Soaking the fabric with water
  • Using a hair dryer or heat to dry spots
