Scotchgard stain protection in Calgary

Is Scotchgard worth it? Here is when stain protection makes sense for your carpet or furniture.

Stain protection

Is Scotchgard worth it?

Scotchgard is relatively expensive, so adding it to a clean is not always worthwhile. If your carpet or upholstery is near the end of its life, save the money for a replacement. It makes sense in these three cases.

  • Your carpet is stain-free and you want a layer of protection in case of accidents
  • Your furniture has sentimental value and you want it to stay presentable
  • Your household is hard on furniture, like a dog on the couch or a toddler with snacks
Great forClean carpetSentimental furniturePet ownersBusy families

Be honest with yourself

When it is worth it and when to skip it

Worth adding

  • Clean carpet or furniture with plenty of life left
  • Pet owners and busy families
  • Furniture you want to keep presentable
  • Light colours that show every spill

Probably skip

  • Carpet near the end of its life
  • Rentals you are about to replace
  • Furniture you plan to replace soon
  • Anything already heavily stained

Scotchgard protection adds 15% to the appointment price. For example, a $300 carpet or upholstery booking would add $45.

Is Scotchgard worth it?

Sometimes. It is worth it on clean carpet, on furniture you want to protect and in homes that are hard on furniture. If your carpet or upholstery is near the end of its life, skip it.
