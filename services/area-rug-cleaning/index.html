Area rug cleaning questions

How much does rug cleaning cost?

Starting prices are $45 for a small rug (3 x 5 ft max), $55 for a medium rug (5 x 8 ft max) and $85 for a large rug (10 x 12 ft max). The minimum service call is $195.

What if I am not sure about the size or material?

Text us a photo and the rough size. We will confirm the details with you before the cleaning.

Can you treat pet odours in rugs?

Yes. We offer pet odour treatment. Choose Heavy Duty for that rug when you book.
