Good to know

Commercial carpet cleaning questions

Can I get an exact price?

Yes. We measure your space in person and multiply by our flat rate. Quotes are free.

Can you come after hours?

Generally yes. Tell us what works for your facility.

Do the doors need to stay open for hoses?

No. Portable commercial-grade extractors mean your building stays secure.

How often should commercial carpet be cleaned?

Every 6 to 12 months, and more often in high traffic areas.

How long does drying take?

Typically about 4 hours. Carpet is only slightly damp to the touch, so wear slippers or clean shoes in the meantime.

Do you move furniture?

Generally no. Small items are fine, but we do not move large furniture.

Why not just pick the cheapest bid?

Check that every quote covers hot water extraction. Cheap quotes for lighter methods often leave carpets dirtier and cost more over time.

Is steam cleaning better than dry cleaning?

It is often more effective at reaching deep-seated dirt.
