Good to know

Pricing questions

Are these the final prices?

They are starting prices. We confirm the final price with you by text before the cleaning, especially if you choose Heavy Duty.

Is there a minimum charge?

Yes, the minimum service call is $195. If your selected items add up to less, the minimum applies.

What is Heavy Duty?

Heavy Duty is for items with pet hair, set-in stains or strong odours. Choose it on the booking page and we will plan extra care.

Can I get a quote before booking?

Yes. Quotes are free with no obligation. Build an estimate online, or call or text with a few details and photos.
