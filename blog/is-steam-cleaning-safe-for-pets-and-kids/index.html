What we use

We use eco-friendly cleaning products and professional all-electric equipment. The result is deep cleaning without the sticky residue that attracts dirt back.

Why it suits families

Dirt, dander and allergens settle into carpet where kids play and pets lie. Removing them makes the floor a healthier place for them to be.

Simple precautions

Keep pets and children out of the room while we work and until the area is dry. Let us know about allergies or sensitivities when you book and we will plan around them.

Quick FAQ

How long until the carpet is dry?
Drying time depends on the material and airflow. A fan or open window helps. We will tell you what to expect for your home.

Can pets stay at home during the clean?
Usually yes, in a different room. Tell us about them when you book.

Are your products really eco-friendly?
Yes. We use cleaning products that are kinder to your family, pets and the environment.
