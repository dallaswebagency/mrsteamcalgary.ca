Gift card FAQ

Before you buy

How do I order a gift card?

Call or text (403) 702-6317 or email MrSteamCalgary@gmail.com with the amount and who it is for. We prepare the card and send it to you.

What can the gift card be used for?

Carpet, upholstery, area rug and mattress cleaning. The recipient books online, or by call or text, and we apply the card.

Is there a minimum or maximum?

Pick any amount you like. Many people choose a room or a sofa, or match one of our starting prices.

Can I buy one for a business or a client?

Yes. Realtors, landlords and property managers often give gift cards. Ask us about bulk gifts.

Do gift cards expire?

Ask us when you order and we will confirm the details in writing with your card.
